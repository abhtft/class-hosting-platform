Upload guidelines
  • Upload your build folder (usually dist or build), made with npm run build. If you drop the whole project, the build folder is found automatically.
  • The folder must contain index.html at its top level.
  • File names: use letters, digits, -, _ and . only. No spaces. Prefer lowercase: Logo.png and logo.png are different files online.
  • Do not include node_modules, .git or .env files.
  • Limits: 2,000 files, 200 MB in total, 50 MB zipped.
  • If your app calls a backend of its own elsewhere, it must use https:// and allow your site's address in CORS. If your teacher enabled Backend for you, you can host a small function here instead and call it at /api/… on your own site (no CORS needed).
  • You can upload again at any time while your token is active. Each upload replaces the previous version.
